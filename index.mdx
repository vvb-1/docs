## Getting Started

Follow these three steps to get up and running with VVB brand assets quickly and correctly.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Review Brand Guidelines">
    Before downloading or using any asset, read through the [Brand Guidelines](/brand-guidelines). Understanding the rules ensures that VVB is represented consistently and professionally across every touchpoint — from a browser tab to a printed document.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Download Your Assets">
    Head to the [Logos](/logos/overview) or [Favicons](/favicons/overview) section and pick the right file for your context. Each page explains which variant to use for large displays, small UI contexts, documents, and specific platforms such as iOS and Android.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Integrate">
    Follow the platform-specific instructions in the [Integration Guide](/usage/web-and-apps) to embed VVB assets correctly in your website, application, or document. Code snippets and configuration examples are provided for the most common use cases.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Automated tools must not redesign VVB logos. Always use the exact approved assets from this library. Modifying, recreating, or reinterpreting any VVB logo file — even programmatically — is not permitted without a formal design approval.
</Note>
