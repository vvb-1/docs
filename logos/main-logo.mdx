## When to Use

- Website hero sections
- Presentation title slides
- Large-format print
- Prominent placements where full logo detail is visible

## When NOT to Use

- Small sizes under ~100px wide (use favicon variant instead)
- Inline document headers (use horizontal logo instead)

<Tip>
  Prefer the SVG for digital use — it scales perfectly at any resolution without quality loss.
</Tip>
